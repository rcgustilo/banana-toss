# Banana Toss

A rooftop banana-throwing artillery game for Android, inspired by the classic QBasic game Gorillas.

## How to play
- Two gorillas stand on buildings in a night-time city.
- Take turns throwing exploding bananas. Set the angle (0–180°, 90 = straight up) and speed (1–100) with the -5, -1, +1, +5 buttons (hold to repeat), tap a number or "Type" to enter exact values, then press THROW. You can also drag on the sky to aim roughly; the throw only happens when you press THROW.
- Gravity and wind (the orange arrow at the bottom of the screen) bend each throw.
- A banana that hits a building blasts a hole in it. A direct hit wins the round.
- Play against the CPU (Easy, Normal or Hard) or with 2 players on one phone.
- Choose the gravity (Earth, Moon, Mars or Jupiter) and how many points win a match (3, 5 or 10).

The APK is built automatically by GitHub Actions. To download it, open the **Releases** page and get `BananaToss.apk`.
